Álvaro Leites

Hello, my name is Álvaro Leites. I am passionate about telecommunications, electronics, computers, science, psychology and music.

I enjoy writing, reading, composing music and continuously learning about different areas of knowledge. This website is, in many ways, a reflection of my personality and of the interests that have accompanied me throughout my life.

My intention is to bring these different interests together: music, philosophy, psychology, science and writing.

Aguas Calmas was born precisely from this combination — as a personal space where ideas, sounds, reflections and experiences can coexist.

I hope you enjoy exploring this website, which has been built with dedication and represents many years of learning, experimentation and personal work.
